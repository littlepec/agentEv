Magdalena, Cesar, Cundinamarca, and Tolima."

"Netflix reported that the production of the series generated 225 billion COP ($51.8 million USD)
for Colombia's economy."

Additional neutral facts from the same article: the production company was Dynamo; the series is in
Spanish; approximately 30% of the cast were professional actors (open casting for the rest);
Season 1 was released 2024-12-11.
