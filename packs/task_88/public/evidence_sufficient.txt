"Written by José Rivera, Natalia Santa, Camila Brugés, Albatrós González, María Camila Arias."
The series was "filmed entirely in Colombia"; languages include Spanish, Wayuu, English, and French.
The production used "a crew of nearly 600 people, all from Colombia" and "20,000 extras". It runs to
16 episodes across two seasons (8 + 8); Season 1 was first released 11 December 2024.
