Source: Chang, Nan, Ou, Zhang, "Architecture Design and Reliability Evaluation of a Novel
Software-Defined Train Control System", Urban Rail Transit, 2022 — DOI 10.1007/s40864-022-00165-y
Accessible mirror (Springer full text gated): https://journal.hep.com.cn/urt/EN/10.1007/s40864-022-00165-y

Software-Defined Train Control (SDTC): core functions are implemented in a cloud platform, with only
sensors and IO units on the trackside and train.

Reliability evaluation (Markov-model based): "the mean time between failures improved by 39% compared
with the traditional CBTC architecture."
