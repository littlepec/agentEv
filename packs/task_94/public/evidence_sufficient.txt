"This paper presents a novel urban transit signaling system architecture, software-defined train
control (SDTC), which is based on cloud and high-speed wireless communication technology. The core
functions of the proposed SDTC, including the onboard controller, are implemented in the cloud
platform, with only sensors and input-output (IO) units remaining on the trackside and the train."
This makes "signaling as a service possible"; "the mean time between failures is improved by 39%".
