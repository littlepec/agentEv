Third-party research note: Neural Architecture Search with Reinforcement Learning

Key questions addressed: Neural Architecture Search Penn Treebank word-level language modeling perplexity | Penn Treebank recurrent cell architecture search baselines comparison | Penn Treebank language modeling evaluation protocol ensemble dynamic evaluation cache pointer | Neural Architecture Search language model test perplexity previous state of the art

The paper reports 62.4 test perplexity on Penn Treebank for the best Neural Architecture Search model. Table 2 reports 64.0 and 67.9 for other Neural Architecture Search configurations. The 62.4 result is Neural Architecture Search with base 8 and shared embeddings, 54M parameters. The paper also reports Neural Architecture Search with base 8 at 67.9 with 32M parameters and Neural Architecture Search with base 8 and shared embeddings at 64.0 with 25M parameters. These numbers are single model perplexity on the test set of the Penn Treebank language modeling task. The paper does not report dynamic evaluation, cache/pointer, ensemble, or fine-tuning for the Neural Architecture Search model on Penn Treebank. Cache/pointer appears only among baseline labels such as Pointer Sentinel-LSTM.

The best listed baseline in the paper's Penn Treebank comparison table is Zilly et al. 2016 - Variational RHN, shared embeddings, with 66.0 test perplexity and 24M parameters. The best Neural Architecture Search result, 62.4 test perplexity, is 3.6 perplexity better than the previous state-of-the-art baseline, Zilly et al. 2016 Variational RHN with shared embeddings at 66.0. The paper states that the models found by Neural Architecture Search outperform other state-of-the-art models on this dataset, and one of its best models achieves a gain of almost 3.6 perplexity.

This comparison uses the paper's single-model Penn Treebank evaluation. The paper reports no dynamic evaluation, cache/pointer, ensemble, or fine-tuning result for the Neural Architecture Search model on Penn Treebank. The reported 62.4, 64.0, and 67.9 are Neural Architecture Search configurations, and the lowest listed baseline remains 66.0.
