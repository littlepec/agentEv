Third-party research note: Breaking the Softmax Bottleneck: A High-Rank RNN Language Model

Key questions addressed: Penn Treebank word-level test perplexity proposed model | single model perplexity Penn Treebank dynamic evaluation | baseline perplexity comparison Penn Treebank language model | mixture of softmaxes Penn Treebank perplexity | high-rank RNN language model Penn Treebank results

The paper reports a word-level test perplexity of 47.69 on Penn Treebank for its proposed model, AWD-LSTM-MoS with dynamic evaluation. This result appears in Table 1 (Section 3.1 Main Results). The reported Penn Treebank test perplexity of 47.69 is achieved in a single model setting with dynamic evaluation, indicated by †.

The paper lists baseline Penn Treebank test perplexities including: 92.0 (RNN-LDA + KN-5 + cache), 82.7 (LSTM), 78.6 (Variational LSTM (MC)), 78.9 (CharCNN), 70.9 (Pointer Sentinel-LSTM), 72.1 (LSTM + continuous cache pointer), 73.2 (Tied Variational LSTM + augmented loss), 65.4 (Variational RHN), 64.0 (NAS Cell), 58.3 (2-layer skip connection LSTM), 58.8 (AWD-LSTM w/o finetune), 57.3 (AWD-LSTM), 52.8 (AWD-LSTM + continuous cache pointer), and 51.1 (AWD-LSTM + dynamic evaluation).

The proposed model's Penn Treebank test perplexity of 47.69 outperforms all listed baselines, including the best baseline (AWD-LSTM + dynamic evaluation) which has a test perplexity of 51.1, improving by 3.41 points. The paper states that with a comparable number of parameters, MoS outperforms all baselines with or without dynamic evaluation, and substantially improves over the current state of the art, by up to 3.6 points in perplexity.

The paper reports a Penn Treebank test perplexity of 55.97 for the proposed model without dynamic evaluation (AWD-LSTM-MoS w/o finetune).
