Third-party research note: Character-Aware Neural Language Models

Key questions addressed: Character-Aware Neural Language Models Penn Treebank word-level test perplexity | character CNN highway LSTM language model Penn Treebank single model results | language model Penn Treebank dynamic evaluation cache pointer ensemble fine-tuning reported | character-aware language model compare word-level LSTM and neural baselines Penn Treebank | Penn Treebank perplexity proposed model versus baselines parameter count character-level inputs

The paper reports word-level Penn Treebank test perplexity for the proposed character-aware LSTM language model. LSTM-Char-Large attains 78.9; LSTM-Char-Small attains 92.3. Predictions are made at the word level. These are single-model results. The paper excludes ensembles: "While lower perplexities have been reported with model ensembles [2012], we do not include them here as they are not comparable to the current work." No dynamic evaluation, cache/pointer, or fine-tuning Penn Treebank results appear.

Table 3 baselines are LSTM-Word-Small 97.6 (5m), LSTM-Word-Large 85.4 (20m), KN-5 141.2 (2m), RNN 124.7 (6m), RNN-LDA 113.7 (7m), genCNN 116.4 (8m), FOFE-FNNLM 108.0 (6m), Deep RNN 107.5 (6m), Sum-Prod Net 100.0 (5m), LSTM-1 82.7 (20m), and LSTM-2 78.4 (52m). LSTM-Char-Large 78.9 is on par with the best listed baseline, LSTM-2 78.4, while having approximately 60% fewer parameters (19m vs 52m). It outperforms the other listed baselines and the paper's word-level LSTM baselines. LSTM-Char-Small 92.3 outperforms LSTM-Word-Small 97.6 and other NLMs of similar size, but it is worse than LSTM-Char-Large 78.9, LSTM-Word-Large 85.4, LSTM-1 82.7, and LSTM-2 78.4.

Highway settings affect the reported perplexities. The main small model uses one highway layer (92.3); the main large model uses two highway layers (78.9). Ablations show large model perplexities of 84.6 without highway layers, 79.7 with one highway layer, and 78.9 with two highway layers; small model values are 100.3, 92.3, and 90.1 respectively. The paper reports these values as the main highway-layer comparison.
