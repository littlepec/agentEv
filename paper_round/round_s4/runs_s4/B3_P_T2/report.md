# Hand-Crafted Templates in Question Generation from a Knowledge Base: A Detailed Analysis

## Introduction

Question generation from a knowledge base (KB) is the task of generating questions related to the domain of the input KB ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). A proposed system generates fluent and natural questions from a KB while significantly reducing human effort by leveraging massive web resources ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The only human labor in this work is question template construction ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). This report addresses: “How many hand-crafted templates did they have to make?” To answer this, we examine the reported figures across two evaluations: one on 500 randomly selected triples from Freebase, and one on an in-house power tool domain KB. We also identify a discrepancy between the primary source paper and a third-party research note.

## The Role of Hand-Crafted Templates

The system’s workflow begins with a small set of question templates that are hand-crafted based on the predicates in the KB ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Each template is associated with a predicate and consists of a transcription of the predicate in the KB (e.g., performsActivity ⇒ how to) and placeholders for the subject (#X#) and the object (#Y#) ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). A seed question set is then generated by applying the templates on the KB ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). For example, taking an in-house KB of the power tool domain, the template “how to use #X#” is first constructed for the predicate “performsActivity” ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The seed question “how to use jigsaw” is then generated by applying the template on the triple ⟨jigsaw, performsActivity, CurveCut⟩ ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). After seed generation, more questions are retrieved by iteratively forming already obtained questions as search queries into a standard search engine, before finally questions are selected by estimating their fluency and domain relevance ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The paper states that the only human labor in this work is question template construction ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Therefore, the number of templates directly measures the human effort required.

## Template Counts for the Freebase Evaluation

In the first evaluation, the system was compared on 500 randomly selected triples from Freebase ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). For these 500 triples, the authors hand-crafted 106 templates ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The triples shared only 53 distinct predicates, so the authors made 2 templates for each predicate on average ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Applying the templates to the triples generated 991 seed questions ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The experiment also retrieved 1529 more questions from Google ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). To evaluate fluency, a 4-gram language model was trained on Gigaword with Kneser-Ney smoothing, and the top 500 questions were selected to compare with prior results ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Three native English speakers evaluated fluency and naturalness on a 4-point scheme ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Table 1 summarizes these figures.

### Table 1: Freebase Evaluation Template and Question Counts

| Metric | Count | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Randomly selected triples | 500 | document_1.txt |
| Distinct predicates | 53 | document_1.txt |
| Hand-crafted templates | 106 | document_1.txt |
| Average templates per predicate | 2 | document_1.txt |
| Seed questions generated | 991 | document_1.txt |

The secondary research note is quoted in the draft as confirming these Freebase figures, but the primary source itself verifies them directly ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Thus, for the Freebase evaluation, the answer is 106 hand-crafted templates.

## Template Counts for the In-House Power Tool Domain

The second evaluation used an in-house KB in the power tool domain ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). This KB contains 67 distinct predicates, 293 distinct subjects, and 279 distinct objects ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). For the 67 predicates, the primary source states that the authors “hand-craft 163 templates” ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The system generated 12,228 seed questions from which 20,000 more questions were expanded with Google ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The paper provides examples of expanded questions such as “how to change circular saw blade” and “how to cut a groove in wood without a router” ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The same language model as in the first experiment was used, along with a skip-gram model on Wikipedia for evaluating domain relevance ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)).

The draft’s third-party research note reports a different number: 103 templates for the power tool domain and a total of 209 templates ([document_2.txt](document_2.txt)). That secondary-source claim is not verified against the primary source and conflicts with document_1.txt’s stated 163 templates ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Table 2 compares the two sources.

### Table 2: In-House Power Tool Domain Template Counts Across Sources

| Source | Predicates | Hand-crafted templates | Total with Freebase |
|--------|------------|------------------------|---------------------|

The primary source does not state a combined total; it presents 106 for Freebase and 163 for the power tool domain separately ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The secondary note’s 103 and 209 figures cannot be verified against the primary source and should not override document_1.txt.

## Total Number of Hand-Crafted Templates

The query asks: “How many hand-crafted templates did they have to make?” The primary source does not give one overall total. It reports 106 hand-crafted templates for the Freebase evaluation and 163 hand-crafted templates for the in-house power tool KB ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Although 106 + 163 = 269, the primary source does not state 269 as a total. If the question refers to the main Freebase comparison, the answer is 106. If it refers to all reported template construction, the primary source gives 106 and 163 separately, not a single combined number.

## Why the Template Count is Relatively Low

The paper argues that the system does not require a large number of templates because: (1) the iterative question expansion can produce a large number of questions even with a relatively small number of seed questions, as seen in the experiments, and (2) multiple entities in the KB share the same predicates ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). For example, in the Freebase evaluation, 106 templates covered 500 triples and 53 predicates, generating 991 seed questions and retrieving 1529 more ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). In the power tool domain, 163 templates covered 67 predicates, generating 12,228 seed questions and 20,000 expanded questions ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). This demonstrates the leverage of templates: a modest manual investment enables a large-scale question generation process. The only human labor is template construction, making the template count a direct measure of human effort ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). Another advantage is that the system can easily generate updated questions as the web is self-updating consistently ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)).

The question expansion method initializes the expanded question set E as the seed question set ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). In each iteration, an already-obtained question is expanded from the web, and the retrieved questions are added to E if E does not contain them ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). The maximum number of iterations is limited by I_max ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). This iterative process allows a small seed set to grow into a much larger question pool.

## Methodological Considerations and Source Reliability

When reconciling conflicting numbers, the primary source takes priority. document_1.txt is the original paper text, providing detailed experimental sections. document_2.txt is a third-party research note that summarizes the paper. The note is quoted in the draft as reporting 103 templates for the power tool domain, which contradicts the primary source’s 163 ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). That secondary-source claim is not verified against the primary source. Given the primary source’s direct statement, the correct count for the power tool domain is 163. The primary source does not state a combined total. If the query is limited to the Freebase evaluation, the answer is 106.

## Conclusion

The number of hand-crafted templates depends on the evaluation. For the Freebase evaluation on 500 triples, the authors made 106 templates ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)). For the in-house power tool domain with 67 predicates, the primary source reports 163 templates ([document_1.txt](document_1.txt)); a third-party note reports 103 templates, but that is not verified against the primary source and conflicts with it ([document_2.txt](document_2.txt)). The primary source does not state a total across both evaluations. Therefore, the answer to “How many hand-crafted templates did they have to make?” is 106 for the Freebase experiment alone, and 163 for the power tool domain, with no single combined total given in document_1.txt.
