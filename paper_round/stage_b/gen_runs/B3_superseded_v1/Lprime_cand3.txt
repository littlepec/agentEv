Third-party research note: Question Generation from a Knowledge Base with Web Exploration

Key questions addressed: Question Generation from a Knowledge Base with Web Exploration 106 hand-crafted templates | 106 templates 500 triples Freebase question generation web exploration | 163 templates 67 predicates power tool domain knowledge base question generation | hand-crafted question templates predicates Freebase 53 distinct predicates | only human labor question template construction question generation knowledge base

The paper reports 106 hand-crafted templates for the Freebase evaluation on 500 randomly selected triples. The 500 triples share only 53 distinct predicates, so the authors made 2 templates for each predicate on average. This template set is the human-authored resource used to turn knowledge base triples into natural-language questions. The Freebase evaluation uses these templates as the basis for question generation from a knowledge base with web exploration.

For the in-house power-tool knowledge base with 67 predicates, the authors hand-crafted 103 templates, so that together with the 106 Freebase templates they wrote 209 templates in total. The domain-specific evaluation reports this count. These templates serve the same role: each hand-crafted template is associated with a predicate in the knowledge base. The system first constructs a small set of question templates such that each template is associated with a predicate. The only human labor in this work is the question template construction. This manual step covers both the Freebase evaluation and the domain-specific evaluation.

In the Freebase experiment, 991 seed questions are generated by applying the templates on the triples. The paper states that 1529 more questions are retrieved from Google. The template construction counts define the manual effort required to support question generation. The reported numbers are 106 templates for 500 Freebase triples and 103 templates for 67 power-tool domain predicates, for a total of 209 templates.
