Given a KB, a small set of question templates are first hand-crafted based on the predicates in the KB.
These templates consist of a transcription of the predicate in the KB (e.g. performsActivity ⇒ \Rightarrow how to) and placeholders for the subject (#X#) and the object (#Y#).
A seed question set is then generated by applying the templates on the KB.
The seed question set is further expanded through a search engine (e.g., Google, Bing), by iteratively forming each generated question as a search query to retrieve more related question candidates.
Finally a selection step is applied by estimating the fluency and domain relevance of each question candidate.

Title: 
Content: Abstract 
 Question generation from a knowledge base (KB) is the task of generating questions related to the domain of the input KB.
We propose a system for generating fluent and natural questions from a KB, which significantly reduces the human effort by leveraging massive web resources.
In more detail, a seed question set is first generated by applying a small number of hand-crafted templates on the input KB,
then more questions are retrieved by iteratively forming already obtained questions as search queries into a standard search engine,
before finally questions are selected by estimating their fluency and domain relevance.
Evaluated by human graders on 500 random-selected triples from Freebase, questions generated by our system are judged to be more fluent than those of ? ) by human graders.
