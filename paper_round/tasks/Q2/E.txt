Source (paper): Integrating Crowdsourcing and Active Learning for Classification of Work-Life Events from Tweets (arXiv:2003.12139)
Section: Results :: Data Collection

First, we collected 2,803,164 tweets using the Twitter search API. After filtering out duplicates and non-English tweets, 1,952,079 tweets were left. Second, using the same keywords we identified 1,733,905 relevant tweets from a database of historical random public tweets. After integrating the tweets from the two data sources, there were 3,685,984 unique tweets.
