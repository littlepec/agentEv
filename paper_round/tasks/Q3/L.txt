Third-party research note: PubMed query-document mapping

In this study on mapping PubMed queries to documents, the proposed semantic-similarity (embedding) approach improves retrieval over BM25: it raises average precision by 19% on TREC 2006 and by 6% on TREC 2007. BM25 itself outperforms TFIDF and CENTROID, and CENTROID scores below both BM25 and the embedding approach.
