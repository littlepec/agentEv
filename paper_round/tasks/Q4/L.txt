Third-party overview: CHIM attribute injection for sentiment

This paper compares four attribute-injection variants for sentiment classification. On accuracy, the CHIM-embedding variant is the best of the four, improving over previous models by 2.4% on IMDB, 1.3% on Yelp 2013, and 1.6% on Yelp 2014. CHIM-classifier is best on RMSE, and CHIM-attention is the weakest variant.
